How this works

Each subject has three kinds of files: PYQ solutions, Notes and Model papers. Click a card to open the PDF. Use the bookmark on any card to keep it under Saved on this device.

To add a PDF, open the repository on GitHub, go to materials/<subject>/<notes | pyq-solutions | model-papers>, choose Add file → Upload files, and commit. It shows up here within a couple of minutes.
